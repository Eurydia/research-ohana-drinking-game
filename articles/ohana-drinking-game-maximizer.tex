\section{Rules}

This is a drinking game involving a standard deck of play cards. The game is played with no less than 6 players and no more than 8 players.

\subsection{No Pointing}

The players must not point with their fingers. Otherwise, they must take a shot as punishment.


\subsection{Card Effects}

The players take turns revealing a card from the deck. Each card has the following effects;

\begin{itemize}
    \item [\textbf{2s}] The player must pick another player. Both must take a shot.
    \item [\textbf{3s}] The player must pick two other players. All three players must take a shot.
    \item [\textbf{4s}] The player on left of the player who revealed the card must take a shot.
    \item [\textbf{5s}] Every player must take a shot.
    \item [\textbf{6s}] The player on the right of the player who revealed the card must take a shot.
    \item [\textbf{7s}] The player must pick another player to battle. The loser becomes a member of \textit{buddy gang}. The player is not allowed to battle a member of the \textit{buddy gang}. If the player who revealed the card is a member of the \textit{buddy gang} and win the battle, they transfer the membership status to the loser. Otherwise, they remain a member. Whenever a member of the \textit{buddy gang} takes a shot, the other members must also take a shot.
    \item [\textbf{8s}] The player may choose to take a 30-second break from the game which pauses all rules. They may also choose to ignore the pause and continue playing.
    \item [\textbf{9s}] Whenever a player draws a nine, every player plays a minigame. The \textit{No Pointing} rule as well as the effects of \textit{Jack}, \textit{Queen}, and \textit{King} are suspended during a minigame.
    \item [\textbf{10s}] The player who draws a ten must cover their face with baby powder.
    \item [\textbf{Jacks}] The player becomes a \textit{Jack}. Only one \textit{Jack} may be active at a given moment. The \textit{Jack} may touch their face. Other players must mimic the \textit{Jack} exactly. The last player to do so must take a shot.
    \item [\textbf{Queens}] The player becomes a \textit{Queen}. Only one \textit{Queen} may be active at a given moment. If a player respond to the \textit{Queen} in any way, they must take a shot.
    \item [\textbf{Kings}] The player becomes a \textit{King}. Only one \textit{King} may be active at a given moment. The \textit{King} must play a role or a character. If the \textit{King} breaks character, they must take a shot.
    \item [\textbf{Aces}] The player must take a shot.



\end{itemize}

\subsection{Minigames}

Across 69 episodes, there have been multiple minigames for the 9s.

\subsubsection{\href{https://www.youtu.be.com/lFEmz4D7Tao}{Pilot episode (Aug 4, 2023)}}


This episode features only minigame for the 9s. That is, whenever a 9 is drawn, the same minigame is played. The minigame is played at at \href{https://youtu.be/lFEmz4D7Tao?t=544}{9:04}, \href{https://youtu.be/lFEmz4D7Tao?t=1237}{20:37}, \href{https://youtu.be/lFEmz4D7Tao?t=1319}{21:59}, and \href{https://youtu.be/lFEmz4D7Tao?t=1472}{24:32}.

\textbf{Word category}: The player who draws the 9 pick a category of their choice and say a word in that category. The next player say a different word in that category. Repeat this process until someone repeats the same word or fail to say a word in time. That player takes a shot. The minigame is over when someone loses. \label{minigame:word-category}

\subsubsection{\href{https://youtu.be/S_QeMzgNCyg}{Ep. 2 (Nov 5, 2023)}}

In this episode, a different minigame is played whenever a 9 is drawn.

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Bottle flip} at \href{https://youtu.be/S_QeMzgNCyg?t=325}{5:25}: Each player is instructed to flip a bottle one by one. Each player receives one chance. After everybody has made their attempt, the gamemaster revealed that whoever successfully flipped the bottle must take a shot.
    \item \textbf{Sing songs with a word} at \href {https://youtu.be/S_QeMzgNCyg?t=534}{8:55}: Each player must sing a song with the word `if' in its lyrics. A player can make up a song but if they get caught, they lose. The loser must take a shot.\label{minigame:sing-song-with-word}
    \item  \textbf{Connecting syllables} at \href{https://youtu.be/S_QeMzgNCyg?t=1068}{17:48}: The player who draws the 9 says a 2-syllable word. The next player must say a different 2-syllable word that starts with the same sound as the final syllable of the previous word. For example, if the first word is `icon', the next word must start with `con.' If a player takes too long to response or repeats a word, they lose and must take a shot.\label{minigame:connecting-syllable}
    \item \textbf{Sing songs with a word} at \href{https://youtu.be/S_QeMzgNCyg?t=1161}{19:21}: The same rule from \ref{minigame:sing-song-with-word} applies except the word is changed to `wake.'
\end{itemize}
